About

Automation is my answer to “what if I could just do everything myself?”

Hi, I'm Reynaldi Wong — call me Rey. I've always been a lazy person, and that laziness made me ask the same question over and over: "What if I could just automate everything?" That question led me to study Mechatronics Engineering.

After graduating I worked with microcontrollers and machines — Arduino, PLCs, Raspberry Pi — and somewhere along the way I found myself doing DevOps. That discovery changed my direction.

This work stays close to what I love: automation, with the ability to do almost everything — though there are plenty of limits when you're handling physical things. Honestly, the title leans more Platform Engineer than DevOps Engineer.

I really enjoy working with Kubernetes, Docker, and Jenkins — watching a deployment pipeline go from manual to fully automated, and finding how a small tweak to a script makes the whole thing faster.

Today I'm a DevOps engineer at Digital Daya Teknologi, focused on improving the deployment pipeline for fintech applications.

You should enjoy the little detours to the fullest. Because that's where you'll find things more important than what you want.
